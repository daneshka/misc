What the number is

The percentage of tested students who met or exceeded the state standard on the 2025 Smarter Balanced assessments, English and maths pooled. Tests run in grades 3–8 and 11, so Elementary is grades 3–5, Middle is 6–8, and High is grade 11 alone. A K–8 school therefore carries both an elementary and a middle score, and switching levels moves it. Schools with fewer than 11 tested students in a band are omitted from that band.

What it isn't

This is a test score, not a school rating. It says nothing about teaching, safety, the arts, or how much a school improves its students — and it tracks neighbourhood income closely, so a high dot partly reflects who lives nearby. Continuation and alternative schools appear as low dots because they enrol students who have already fallen behind; that's their job, and the number reads them unfairly. Grade 11 is a single cohort, so high schools bounce year to year.

Online charters are excluded — they have addresses but no catchment, and several San Diego districts authorise ones physically located in Simi Valley, Irvine and Redlands. Nine state-authorised charters (High Tech High and similar) sit inside no district but are mapped at their real campuses.
