Walk north → south, and start early. The tide-sensitive spots on this route — the rocky bluff pinch points below Encinitas and Solana Beach — sit in the southern half of the walk. With low tide at 9 AM and the tide climbing all afternoon to a 4.94 ft high at 4 PM, you want to clear those bluffs before about 1–2 PM, while the water is still low enough to leave dry sand at the cliff base.

A 6:30 AM start puts you on the easy, wide-open Carlsbad beaches during the lowest water, and gets you through the Cardiff & Solana Beach bluffs around midday. Sleep in and start at 10–11 AM instead, and you'll hit those same bluffs near peak high tide — likely cut off and forced up to Highway 101.

Know before you go

⚠ The two real risks

  • Lagoon mouths after rain. San Elijo especially can run too deep to wade no matter the tide. June is normally bone-dry in San Diego, but check a recent local surf/trail report before committing.
  • Getting pinched at the bluffs. If you fall badly behind and hit Cardiff/Solana Beach after ~2–3 PM on a rising tide, expect to detour up to Highway 101 in spots. Not dangerous if you respect the sets — just less of a pure beach walk.

🎒 Pack list

  • Water — at least 2 L; refill at Carlsbad Village & South Carlsbad.
  • Sun protection — the marine layer burns off by mid-morning and there's zero shade.
  • Shoes you can wade in, or sandals — every lagoon crossing means wet feet.
  • Phone in a dry bag; cash/card for the food stops.
  • Light layer for the cool, foggy start.

🚆 Getting back

  • The COASTER commuter train runs the same corridor, with stations at Oceanside, Carlsbad, Encinitas, and Solana Beach (nearest stop to Del Mar).
  • Easiest plan: park near Solana Beach / Del Mar, ride the train up to Oceanside in the morning, then walk home south.
  • Check the COASTER weekend schedule in advance — Sunday service is limited.

⏱ If you start later

  • Shift every time on the timeline by the same amount.
  • Starting after ~8:30 AM means hitting the Cardiff/Solana bluffs past 3 PM near peak high tide — plan on road detours there.
  • Earlier is always safer on this route. There's no penalty for a 6 AM start except the alarm clock.
